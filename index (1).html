Camp built by people who grew up in these woods.

Forests Today, Forests Tomorrow was founded on a simple idea: kids who spend real time outdoors — not a single field trip, but days and weeks of it — come away different. More confident, more curious, and a lot more likely to still care about the outdoors as adults.

Our counselors are trained naturalists and educators, our sessions run rain or shine, and every activity is designed to work on two levels: a great day right now, and a habit of getting outside that lasts long after camp ends.
